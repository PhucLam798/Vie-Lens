### OPS-6 — 2026-09-29 — Snapshot completeness criterion (decided by: human)
An index's `metadata.total_size` is not used as a completeness gate: upstream indexes can disagree with their own shards (Qwen3-4B-Base @ 906bfd4b: index 8,045,591,552 vs shards 8,044,982,000, which equal the Hub's LFS sizes). A snapshot is complete when every weight file resolves to a non-`.incomplete` blob named by its 64-hex sha256, and each safetensors file's size equals 8 + header length + the largest tensor end offset in its header, with every tensor the index assigns to that shard present in the header. Only headers are read; tensor data is never read.

### OPS-7 — 2026-09-29 — E1 lens-validity criterion (decided by: human)
The model computes its logits in bf16, whereas the analysis lens is fp32, so the two cannot agree to 1e-3 (T04a on tiny models: about 2e-3). E1 therefore gates on the **model-dtype lens path**: the model's own final norm and unembedding applied to the hooked block-N output must reproduce the model logits with max |Δ log-prob| < 1e-3 (expected exact) and 100% top-1 agreement. For the fp32 analysis lens, the max |Δ log-prob| and top-1 agreement against the model logits are reported, not gated. This supersedes the wording of E1 check 2 in `docs/experiments.md` and of AGENTS.md rule 36.

### OPS-14 — 2026-10-02 — E2–E5 analysis choices and Checkpoint H (decided by: human)
Decided after reviewing `docs/analysis_spec.md` (T13a, `db5ee40`) and the A6 gate on P-D (non-test) data, before any test-item result was read.
1. Layers: every `max_ℓ` is over block outputs ℓ = 1..N (PREREG Effective §5.2); ℓ = 0 is excluded.
2. H2 band: `M_L = max_ℓ rise_L` for L ∈ {vi, en, zh, fr, id}. The band holds iff `min_L M_L > 0` and `max_L M_L / min_L M_L ≤ 1.5`.
3. H1 rise criterion: `Q_H1 = M_en − 2·mean(M_fr, M_id)`; it holds iff `Q_H1 ≥ 0`. The ratio `M_en / mean(M_fr, M_id)` is reported when its denominator is > 0.
4. LA: `h̃ = h − μ̄` (μ̄ = mean of the five fit-split class means, per family × format × layer); `v_L = μ_L − μ̄`. `LA_L(c,ℓ) = (cos(h̃, v_L) − c_rand(ℓ)) / (c_nat,L(ℓ) − c_rand(ℓ))`, and `LA_L(ℓ)` is its mean over query concepts. `c_nat,L(ℓ)` = mean cosine over the validate prompts of language L. `c_rand(ℓ)` = mean cosine of the centered Vietnamese query activations with 1,000 seeded unit Gaussian directions, drawn independently per layer with stream key `la_random|<family>|<format>|<layer>` under the `derived/seeds.json` scheme. If `c_nat,L(ℓ) − c_rand(ℓ) ≤ 0` for any L, LA at that layer is undefined, and the layer is flagged and treated as a failed layer.
5. LA query prompts: all main test prompts of the matching format (repetition 1,535; translation 1,524). Cloze is descriptive and uses the repetition directions.
6. Gate and failed layers: a validate prompt is correct iff `argmax_L cos(h̃, v_L)` over the five languages is its language; a layer passes iff ≥ 124 of 130. Failed (and undefined) layers are excluded from every `max_ℓ LA`, from the `D_en` scalar and from the `D_zh` window, and are flagged. The maximum over all layers is reported as a sensitivity, with a statement of whether including the failed layers would change any rule outcome. Only if no layer passes for a family × format is LA "not evaluable" there; its H1/H2 outcome is then "inconclusive".
7. `D_en`: the curve with pointwise CIs; the scalar is the mean over passing layers with 0.25 ≤ ℓ/N ≤ 0.75.
8. `L_concept` (frozen, A2): `p_L(ℓ) = exp(mean_c s(c,L,ℓ))`; anchor `p_vi(N)`; readouts {vi, en, zh, fr, id}; thresholds 0.10 (primary), 0.05 and 0.20.
9. Verdicts: the rules are applied per format (repetition, translation; cloze excluded). A model supports H1 (or H2) only if both formats meet that rule; otherwise it is "inconclusive", with the per-format pattern stated (protocol §7.5).
10. Holm family (a): the T13a proposal (test of `M_L > 0`) is degenerate, because `rise_L` averages exactly 0 over `ℓ_early`, so `M_L ≥ 0` in every draw. Instead, per format and per model × L ∈ {vi, en, zh, fr, id}: `W_L` = mean of `rise_L(ℓ)` over blocks with 0.25 ≤ ℓ/N ≤ 0.75 (the A11 window); H0: `W_L ≤ 0`; `p = (1 + #{b : W_L^(b) ≤ 0}) / (1 + B)`; Holm over the 15 tests within each format. `M_L` remains the decision-rule quantity, reported with its CI.
11. Bootstrap: B = 1,000, 95% percentile CIs. One concept-index draw per (item set, model, format), key `bootstrap|<item_set>|<model>|<format>` under the `derived/seeds.json` scheme, shared by all languages and quantities on that item set. Item sets:
    - `cf729`: cognate-free; 729 repetition / 721 translation;
    - `main`: main concepts with a prompt in that format; 1,535 / 1,524;
    - `m1_main`: M1-eligible main;
    - `m1_all`: M1-eligible main + extension; descriptive.

    Only test (query) concepts are resampled: the fitted directions, μ̄, `c_nat` and `c_rand` stay fixed, and reports state that the CIs exclude direction-estimation uncertainty. A statistic undefined in a draw (for example, no crossing) is reported with its valid-draw count, and its CI is taken over the valid draws.
12. Landmarks: primary on `cf729` (the rise-rule items); `main` is the R9 sensitivity. On ties, take the earliest layer. If no layer after `L_peak` has `rise_vi > rise_pivot`, `L_cross` = NA ("no crossing") and pivot width = NA. Pivot width uses `L_concept` at 0.10.
13. C1 (descriptive; no rule, no Holm), per model × format (cloze descriptive):
    - **Instrument effect:** `L_concept^M2 − L_concept^M1` (in blocks and in ℓ/N) on `m1_main`. `L_concept^M1` uses `p_L^M1(ℓ) = mean_c P_M1(c,L,ℓ)` with anchor `p_vi^M1(N)` and the same thresholds.
    - **Selection effect:** `L_concept^M2(m1_main) − L_concept^M2(main)`, paired on the `main` draw: the subset is the drawn concepts that are M1-eligible.
    - **Extension:** the same instrument comparison on `m1_all`, reported as a separate descriptive line. No rise-based landmark is computed on extension items, which have no distractors (OPS-13).
14. E2 token sets: epfl-dlab `process_tokens`. Every non-empty character prefix of the target, bare and with the tokenizer's word-start marker (`▁` Gemma; `Ġ` Llama, Qwen), deduplicated. For zh (and ru, descriptive) add the token of the first UTF-8 byte of the first character, resolved through the tokenizer's own vocabulary: SentencePiece byte fallback `<0xHH>` for Gemma, the byte-level BPE byte-to-unicode symbol for Llama and Qwen. If it is absent, nothing is added and the item is counted. M1 readouts are {vi, en, zh no-space source form, fr, id}; `P_M1` is the summed fp32 full-vocabulary softmax at the answer position.
15. FLORES transfer pooling: `last` primary; `mean` reported.
16. H3 `D_zh` (E6, after Checkpoint H): mean over passing layers with 0.25 ≤ ℓ/N ≤ 0.75; stratified concept bootstrap.
17. A4: rule outcomes and `L_cross` are also computed with `s¹`. If the verdict or `L_cross` differs between `s` and `s¹`, both are reported.
18. Checkpoint H: the agent writes `reports/adjudication_draft.md`. The human finalises it as `reports/adjudication.md` and commits it (rule 30).

### D1 — Matched-distractor Δ replaces per-language z (A1)
Δ(c,L,ℓ) = s(c,L,ℓ) − s(d|c,L,ℓ), in nats. rise_L(ℓ) = mean_c Δ(c,L,ℓ) − mean_c mean_{ℓ'∈ℓ_early} Δ(c,L,ℓ'). The H1/H2 rules apply to max_ℓ rise_L. The v1.1 z-statistic is appendix-only. One distractor per (model family, concept, readout language, target variant): another main-test concept with the same number of content tokens for L in that family, a different first content token, and the nearest `zipf_en`; exact ties are broken by a seeded draw (seed 20260925). Distractors are teacher-forced after c's prompt. Pool and tie-break details are fixed in the T03b manifest spec and frozen in `derived/` before any run.

### D2 — `L_concept` anchored to the Vietnamese answer (A2)
The first ℓ at which p_L(ℓ) ≥ 0.10 · p_vi(N) for any readout language L, with p = exp(s) on concept-mean curves. 5% and 20% are sensitivities.

### D3 — Chinese target without the leading space is primary (A3)
The released with-space form is a sensitivity. Latin-script targets keep their space. X0c-a showed 0 prefix violations for the no-space form in all six tokenizers.

### D4 — H1/H2 rules on the cognate-free subset (A5)
729 main-test concepts (sha256 of the sorted id list `f1e7a6b5a0c197b1769215224503ee33a8f2e216943ae0d8088d55219042a58f`): en–fr and en–id surface distance ≥ 0.45 **and** five distinct first content tokens under all three tokenizers. All items are sensitivity R9; H3 uses the full strata.

### D5 — H3 frequency covariate (A8)

### D6 — Steering design (A7), as specified
Translation-format set-1 main items; up to 300 competent concepts per model, seeded and stratum-proportional; u_ℓ = μ_en − μ_vi (translation directions, fit split); injection at block output at the last prompt position and at generated positions; k ∈ {0.5, 1, 2}; a random same-norm direction as control; greedy generation, stopping at `\n`, `" -"` or 8 tokens; outcome classes VI / EN / OTHER-LANG / DAMAGE; the continuous log P(en) − log P(vi); coincidence with `L_cross` at ±0.10 depth with the CI of the difference including 0, reported in blocks and depth. The E8 sampling rule is frozen in `derived/` (T03b).
